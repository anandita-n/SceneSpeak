About communication boards

What is a communication board?

A communication board is a grid of picture symbols a child can point to — instead of, or alongside, speaking. It's one form of AAC (Augmentative and Alternative Communication).

A board built around one place or activity gives a child exactly the words they need in that moment: at the playground, "swing", "push" and "my turn" matter far more than a general list of words.

Who it's for

  • Autistic children
  • Nonverbal and minimally verbal children
  • Children with Down syndrome or other speech and language needs
  • Children with selective mutism or anxiety
  • Children learning English as an additional language

How SceneSpeak works

  1. 1

    Upload a photo

    Any picture of the child's surroundings — the playground, the snack table, bath time.

  2. 2

    Get a board

    Words for what's in the photo, each with a symbol, in sentence order: people, actions, places, describing words, things. Words the child already knows keep the same wording.

  3. 3

    Tap and talk

    Each tile speaks its word aloud. Remove any word that doesn't fit before you start.
