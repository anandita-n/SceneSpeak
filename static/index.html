SceneSpeak

Accessible communication, generated instantly

AAC communication boards, built from a photo

SceneSpeak helps speech-language pathologists, educators, and caregivers create personalized communication boards for autistic and nonverbal children — in seconds, from a real photo of the child's own environment.

Every board is grounded in standardized AAC vocabulary, checked against the photo itself so suggested words actually match what's there, and mapped to recognized AAC symbols — something you can hand to a child with confidence, not just something that looks plausible.
